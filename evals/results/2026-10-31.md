## Behavioral smoke checks

Separate evaluator agents read the baseline and candidate files. The baseline ran supplier-contradiction and proposal-capacity; the candidate ran those plus management-cash-gap. Each evaluator processed its cases separately within one evaluation session, sharing only common instructions. No real client data or external services were used. Delegation by evaluators was prohibited.

Model inherited from this Codex session, with no model override; exact provider model version, reasoning configuration, usage, latency and correction time were not captured. These are unblinded single-run observations, not the controlled repeated benchmark in the evaluation protocol.

| Case | Baseline observation | Candidate observation |
|---|---|---|
| supplier-contradiction | Flagged missing/current recovery evidence and unknown financial resilience; loaded enterprise-risk, internal-audit and sox-icfr | Preserved those judgments with stable evidence/action IDs and separate review axes; loaded only operations-supply-chain |
| proposal-capacity | Calculated CAD 12,000 labor and CAD 2,000 gap; flagged French-capacity gap and blocked firm offer | Preserved arithmetic and language/budget gaps; used only proposal-business-development and explicit requirement statuses |
| management-cash-gap | Not run | Computed +20% revenue, +12.5% unfavorable costs and +50% profit; found closing cash 30 versus expected 25 (CAD thousands), retained campaign attribution as unverified |

The initial candidate proposal silently treated ambiguous weekly reports as progress updates; baseline explicitly labeled that assumption. The workflow was tightened to require the exact interpretation in the requirement matrix and provisional status. The final independent reviewer reran the proposal case and explicitly kept R1 provisional, distinguishing operational finance reports from project updates. The reviewer found no important implementation issues. That rerun occurred within the review context, not a fresh-context release benchmark.

Seventeen other cases are supplied but were not run. No superiority over AI Hero or professional validation is claimed.
