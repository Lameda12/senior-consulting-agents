1. Identify the practice(s) and select 1-3 relevant specialist personas (plugin subagents, or persona files in the `senior-consulting-agents` skill).
2. Confirm scope, jurisdiction, framework and whether the client is an audit client (independence).
3. Delegate to the agents, then consolidate into a single partner-ready deliverable with an executive summary, prioritised findings and next steps.
