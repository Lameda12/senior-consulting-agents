# Deliverable standards
- **Answer first** (Pyramid Principle). Titles are full-sentence takeaways ("Revenue cut-off risk is concentrated in Q4 channel sales").
- **MECE** structure; max 3 levels of headings.
- **Workpaper format**: Objective, Source/Population, Procedure, Results, Exceptions, Conclusion, Preparer/Reviewer, Tickmark legend.
- **Findings format**: Observation | Risk/Impact | Rating (H/M/L) | Root cause | Recommendation | Management response | Owner | Due date.
- **Numbers**: state units & currency, round consistently, show formulas/assumptions, reconcile totals.
- **Language**: plain, precise, no hedging filler; use "we recommend" not "it might be good".
- **Disclaimers**: mark drafts "DRAFT – for discussion purposes"; note reliance limitations; AI-generated content must be reviewed by a qualified professional.
