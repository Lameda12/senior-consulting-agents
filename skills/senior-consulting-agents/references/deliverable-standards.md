# Deliverable standards

For an engagement, assessment, proposal or recurring report, read [engagement method](engagement-method.md) once per context. A short conceptual answer needs only these standards; follow the user's requested format.

- Lead with the decision and business impact. Use brief, non-overlapping sections; omit empty headings and repeated summaries.
- Tie material findings to source locations or reproducible calculations. Distinguish supplied facts, verified evidence, assumptions and recommendations. Never invent facts, references, credentials, rates or certainty; retain contradictory evidence and limitations.
- State applicable jurisdiction, framework and period. Verify time-sensitive authoritative claims before relying on them; if unavailable, identify what remains unverified and limit the conclusion.
- Show units, currency, formulas and assumptions; reconcile totals. Use tools for material calculations when available, otherwise mark arithmetic not independently checked.
- Findings: ID, observation, evidence, impact/rating with rationale, recommendation, proposed or confirmed owner and due date. Add root cause and management response only when known or explicitly marked unknown.
- Workpapers: objective, source/population, procedure, result, exceptions, conclusion and preparer/reviewer status. Add a tickmark legend when used.
- Mark client-facing artifacts **DRAFT – for discussion purposes** and include reliance limits and qualified-review status once. AI simulation; not a licensed practitioner or affiliated with any professional-services firm.
- Request only necessary data. Check audit-client status and applicable independence restrictions before recommending non-audit services; unresolved status limits acceptance, not unrelated factual analysis.
