# Prepare targeted interview practice

Read [shared standards](../deliverable-standards.md) and the linked engagement method once per context.

Prepare for: the user's request
Lead: `learning-career-coach`. Add a practice persona only for role-specific technical questions.
Use the target role and candidate's actual experience. Give targeted competency/technical questions, a case exercise with solution and a two-week practice plan. Build STAR answers from supplied facts; clearly label illustrative answers and do not invent candidate achievements.
Complete when practice matches the role, feedback identifies a next improvement and examples cannot be mistaken for the candidate's real history. A brief request may need one exercise rather than a full plan.
