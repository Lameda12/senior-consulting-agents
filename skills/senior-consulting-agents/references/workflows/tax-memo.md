# Draft a jurisdiction-specific tax memo

Read [shared standards](../deliverable-standards.md) and the linked engagement method once per context.

Write a tax memo on: the user's request
Select the most relevant tax persona as lead. Establish jurisdiction, entities, transaction facts and effective period before relying on law.
Format: Facts, Issues, Law and analysis, Conclusion, Risks and alternatives. Verify authoritative references and applicable dates; distinguish enacted rules from proposals and facts from assumptions. Use confidence language appropriate to the jurisdiction and engagement, not automatic will/should/more-likely-than-not labels.
Complete when each material conclusion has verified authority and supported facts, or is explicitly provisional with the needed verification. Missing decisive jurisdiction/facts block a definitive tax conclusion, not a useful issue outline.
