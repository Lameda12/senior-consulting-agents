# Assess risks, controls and remediation

Read [shared standards](../deliverable-standards.md) and the linked engagement method once per context.

Assess: the user's request
Lead: `enterprise-risk`. Add `internal-audit` for control testing, `sox-icfr` only for financial-reporting control scope. For a supplier-specific decision use `supplier-risk` instead.
Define business objectives and approved likelihood/impact criteria. Distinguish inherent and residual risk; do not reduce residual ratings without evidence of mitigation. Keep unknown likelihood/control operation explicit rather than inventing scores.
Deliver an evidence-linked risk register, control mapping, gaps and remediation roadmap. Complete when each material risk has a defensible rating, control evidence or gap, proposed owner, deadline and closure criterion.
